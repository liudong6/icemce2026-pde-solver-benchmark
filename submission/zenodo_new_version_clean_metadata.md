Title: A Coefficient-Aware Finite-Difference Benchmark for Solver Selection and CPU/GPU Stencil Scaling in Heat-Conduction Simulation

Creator: Dong Liu; University of Nottingham Ningbo China

Version: v5

Date: 2026-09-21

This archive contains the manuscript, source code, raw CSV evidence, generated figures and tables, environment requirements, and cold-start smoke-test log for a reproducible finite-difference benchmark of solver selection and CPU/GPU stencil scaling in heat-conduction simulation. The study verifies a conservative finite-difference operator, evaluates coefficient-aware solver decisions for CG, Jacobi-PCG, and AMG-PCG, and reports bounded CPU/Numba/CUDA stencil measurements on one workstation. This revision adds an exact matched-field and forcing audit with translation controls, an independent motif family, retained pilot failures, fresh-setup timings and preconditioned spectral diagnostics. This version includes both the retained baseline and the new experiments. A further 744-solve candidate-solver audit, including 504 interleaved confirmation solves, tests classical AMG and identifies portfolio-dependent translation reversals. The prior published baseline and extension are identified by 10.5281/zenodo.22668106; the original DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22303525 identifies baseline data only. This revision adds 378 paired callback-calibration solves and complete main-text face-averaging and timing-protocol evidence. The complete revised archive is identified by 10.5281/zenodo.22874266. The manuscript highlights changes in yellow relative to the previous reviewed version.

Public status and checksums are verified in a separate publication receipt. Earlier published versions are preserved. No conference submission is performed.
